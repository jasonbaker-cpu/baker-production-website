Questions owners ask

Is the price estimate really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation to list with us. Most owners use it to plan, whether they sell next month or in two years.

How do you work out the price?

We compare your home with homes that have recently sold and are under offer nearby, allow for its size, condition and land, and check it against the buyers on our list who are looking in your area right now.

Do you need to come through the house?

Not for a first estimate. We can give you a range by phone. For a sharper number, a 20-minute walk-through makes a real difference, and we'll only book one if you want it.

How long does it take to sell?

Our homes have taken 23 days on average over the last 12 months. Every home and market is different, and we'll give you an honest view for yours.

Will you share my details?

No. Your details go to Jayden and James only, and we only contact you about your property.
