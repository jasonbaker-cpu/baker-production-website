This is the only standard for Baker day and twilight photos. 15 rules and 18 reference photos. It replaces every earlier email, note, review and reference link.

Colour comes first

  1. True to the house first, bright second. Floors, walls, cabinets, benchtops, carpet, pavers, brick and garden stay the colour of the real house. If you must choose between brighter and accurate, choose accurate.
  2. The flash frame is the colour reference. Every interior scene has one flash frame. Match wall, floor, cabinet and timber colour to it. Do not use the white balance eyedropper on walls.
  3. Cream walls stay cream. Grey cabinets stay grey. Timber stays timber. Never turn a room white to make it look clean. Removing a yellow lamp cast from a white wall is correct.

Interiors

  1. Bright but not hazy. TVs, pendants, benchtops and dark furniture stay black. Soft shadows under furniture stay. Never brighter than the reference photos.
  2. Windows pulled. The view is visible and natural. No halos or glowing edges.
  3. No extra clarity, texture, dehaze, sharpening or saturation. If unsure, do less.
  4. Straight verticals on every photo.
  5. The whole set matches. Same brightness and colour in every photo of the job. If one photo is off, fix the look of the whole set.

Exteriors

  1. Natural, like the raw. No HDR look. Greens natural, not neon. Pavers, brick and roof their real colour, nothing glowing. Do not lift shadows until the photo looks flat.
  2. Day sky: soft pale blue, near white at the horizon, a few light clouds. Never deep blue, grey or white.
  3. No cut lines. After a sky replacement, check every tree, palm and roof edge. No white outline or halo against the sky.
  4. Never remove neighbouring houses or power lines.

Twilight

  1. Natural sunset, lights on. House and lawn stay bright. Soft pink and purple are fine. No dark fake purple, no all-gold sky, no grey cloud tops.
  2. Sky from the Default folder only, and a different sky on every job. Grey or white houses get a blue and pink sky, not orange.

Delivery

  1. Edit every photo in the folder. No duplicate angles. Same size and same file names. Follow the booking notes for the job.

If you are unsure: match the flash frame and the reference photos below. If a message from Baker ever disagrees with this page, this page wins. Ask Jason before you edit. Fixes on one job are fixes for that job only. They do not change these rules.

Editor tools

Self-check my job

Before you deliver, drop your finished photos here. You get a report straight away: score, which photos to fix and why, and duplicates. Add the same-named raw files (0EV .ARW) for at least three rooms and the tool compares your colour with the real house. It never asks for more HDR, saturation, sharpening or shadow lift. Strict check: deliver only when it shows zero flagged photos, zero duplicates and zero set issues, and you've done every eye check. One flag = not ready. Works for day and twilight/dusk sets. Photos stay on your computer and are not uploaded.

Drone

Drone

  • Horizon level. Always replace the sky with a soft pale blue sky, fading to near-white at the horizon, light wispy clouds. Never deep or saturated blue.
  • Location shots: 3 pins, all the same small size. Labels never cover pins. Never write "zoned" on schools.
  • House-only close-ups: no pins.
  • Two top-downs per job: one with the road at the bottom, one with the road on the side.
  • Top-down: lot line follows the fence exactly, bold white lot size with a proper ² (e.g. 678m²), blur everything outside the lot, property centred, no logo.
  • Same file size across the whole set.

Premium day photos

Premium tier only. Standard jobs keep the normal rules above.

Premium look

  • Natural light only. Ambient HDR brackets, no flash, property lights off. It should look like the room on a nice day.
  • Bright overall. No super-dark corners. Lift the far side of the room so it still reads clearly.
  • Keep soft shadows. Light falls off gently from the windows. Don't flatten every shadow like a standard job.
  • Warm-neutral whites. Cream white, never grey/blue, never yellow.
  • True blacks. Black tiles, splashbacks and frames stay black, not grey.
  • Windows like DUET Property's natural-light photos (from 9 Nov). The view stays visible with its real colour (sunlit garden, sky), slightly brighter than the room like real daylight. Never grey or flat, never darkened to match the room, no halos. Window frames crisp and dark.

Window reference: DUET Property (lights off)

Open duetproperty.com.au and look at the listings shot with the lights off. Warm natural light, the view clearly visible with real colour, a touch brighter than the room. Match that on every premium window.

Premium vs standard

  • Standard: follow the 15 rules and 18 reference photos at the top of this page.
  • Premium: just as bright in the main area, but with natural depth and direction of light.
  • Same set rule: every photo in the job matches.
  • Exteriors and drone: same rules as standard (soft pale blue sky, natural greens and shadows that match the raw).
  • Benchmark: 4 McGlew Rd, Glen Forrest.
