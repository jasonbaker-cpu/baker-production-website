Job reviews

Latest QC on our jobs. Fix the red points; keep doing the green ones.

Rules

Interiors

  • Walls warm white. Not grey/blue, not yellow/cream, not blown to pure white.
  • Bright, not washed out. Rooms light and airy like Thorburn; ceilings must keep detail.
  • Ceilings read white. No orange or peach cast.
  • Every photo in a job matches in brightness and colour, including patios and alfrescos.
  • Windows pulled with the view visible. No halos or glowing edges.
  • Verticals straight.
  • Timber floors natural. Not orange or over-saturated.

Exteriors & skies

  • Day skies soft pale blue. Never deep/dark blue, never grey/white.
  • Twilight: natural sunset look, lights on. Soft pink/purple OK; dark fake purple is not.
  • Lift shadows under trees and patios. No harsh black shadows.
  • Grass natural green, not lime.
  • Never remove neighbouring houses or power lines.

Drone

  • Horizon level. Always replace the sky with soft pale blue.
  • Location shots: 3 pins, all the same small size. Labels never cover pins. Never write "zoned" on schools.
  • House-only close-ups: no pins.
  • Two top-downs per job: one with the road at the bottom, one with the road on the side.
  • Top-down: lot line follows the fence exactly, bold white lot size with a proper ² (e.g. 678m²), blur everything outside the lot, property centred, no logo.
  • Same file size across the whole set.

Delivery

  • No duplicates. Don't send two photos of the same room from the same angle.
  • All photos same resolution.
  • Redo the whole set's look if one photo is off. Don't fix only one and leave the rest.
  • Follow the booking notes for each job.

Premium day photos

Premium tier only. Standard jobs keep the normal rules above.

Premium look

  • Natural light only. Ambient HDR brackets, no flash, property lights off. It should look like the room on a nice day.
  • Bright overall. No super-dark corners. Lift the far side of the room so it still reads clearly.
  • Keep soft shadows. Light falls off gently from the windows. Don't flatten every shadow like a standard job.
  • Warm-neutral whites. Cream white, never grey/blue, never yellow.
  • True blacks. Black tiles, splashbacks and frames stay black, not grey.
  • Window pull on every frame. View visible, no halos.

Premium vs standard

  • Standard: bright, even, walls ~210, very few shadows.
  • Premium: just as bright in the main area, but with natural depth and direction of light.
  • Same set rule: every photo in the job matches.
  • Exteriors and drone: same rules as standard (soft pale blue sky, lifted shadows).
  • Benchmark: 4 McGlew Rd, Glen Forrest.
