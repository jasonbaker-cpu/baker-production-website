Baker Production photographs real estate across Perth's northern suburbs, from Scarborough, Trigg, North Beach and Sorrento through Duncraig, Joondalup and Kinross to Clarkson, Butler and Alkimos. Sales shoots start at $189 and packages at $299, delivered next day. Alkimos is the northern edge of the standard coverage area.

What shooting the northern suburbs actually involves

The northern corridor is really two markets sharing a freeway. From Trigg and North Beach down through Scarborough and Sorrento you have established coastal stock where the ocean is the product — and the ocean does not photograph from a living room. Drone is close to mandatory on anything with a glimpse, because that glimpse is what the price is built on.

North of Joondalup the character changes completely. Clarkson, Butler and Alkimos are volume markets: newer builds, tighter blocks, faster turnover, and agents running several listings at once rather than one campaign at a time. What matters there is not artistry but a supplier who turns up when they said, shoots the whole property in ninety minutes, and has the gallery back before the listing goes live.

In between sits Duncraig, Kingsley, Woodvale, Padbury and Carine — family stock on decent blocks where a floor plan does more selling than a twelfth photograph of the same living area. The Premium package at $375 is the right shape for most of it.
