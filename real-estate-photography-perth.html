Photography that makes a Perth listing look worth the ask

Buyers decide in the scroll. A listing on realestate.com.au gets a second and a half of attention before someone swipes past, and the only thing doing the work in that second and a half is the lead image. Baker Production shoots property for Perth agents who understand that and want it done properly, every time, without chasing.

We have shot more than 1,500 properties across the metro area — character homes in Mount Lawley, coastal builds in Trigg and North Beach, new estate stock through Baldivis and Ellenbrook, and the big western-suburbs listings where the photography has to carry a seven-figure price. We know which rooms sell a home, which angle flatters a narrow block, and what time of day a west-facing rear elevation actually looks good.

Every shoot is edited to a consistent house standard: straight verticals, natural colour, windows that are not blown out, and lawns that look like lawns. Nothing that will get flagged by a portal, nothing that looks like a different house when the buyer walks in.
