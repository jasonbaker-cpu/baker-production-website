Rental property photography in Perth costs $159 with Baker Production — up to 20 wide edited images, 30 minutes on site, delivered next day. Property managers can book multiple properties into a single scheduled run. Floor plans, decluttering and drone can be added. Prices include GST.

A rental listing is a volume business

A property manager is not marketing one home beautifully. They are turning over a portfolio, week after week, and what they need is a supplier who turns up, shoots it properly, and has the images back before the listing needs to go live. Not artistry — reliability.

The rental shoot is built for exactly that: 30 minutes on site, up to 20 wide images, every room covered, delivered the next day at $159. It is priced below a sales shoot because it is a different job, not a discounted version of the same one.

If you are running several properties at once, we schedule them as a run rather than as separate visits, which is faster for us and cheaper in dead time for you.
