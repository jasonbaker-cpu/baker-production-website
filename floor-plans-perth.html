Home/Floor Plans Perth

A real estate floor plan in Perth costs $159 for a property up to 200m², $199 for 200–300m² and $239 for 300–500m² with Baker Production. Redrawing an existing plan is $30, or $45 including a site plan. Plans are measured on site during the shoot and delivered the next day. Prices include GST.

The second thing a buyer looks at, after the front photo

A buyer scrolling realestate.com.au looks at the lead image, then the floor plan. The photos tell them whether they like it; the plan tells them whether it works. Bedrooms away from the living area, a second living space, whether the kitchen looks over the yard — those are inspection-or-not decisions, and a listing without a plan loses them by default.

Baker floor plans are measured on the day of the shoot, not traced from a builder's original. That matters in Perth, where a large share of stock has been extended, re-walled or renovated since it was drawn. What we deliver is the house as it stands.

The output is clean black and white — the format portals, print and window cards all handle without complaint — with room labels and dimensions, sized for both web and print.
