About

Perth Property Media, Done Right.

Baker Production is a small Perth-based studio producing photography, video, drone, floor plans, and virtual editing for property listings. We work mostly with real estate agents across the metro area, plus the occasional homeowner who wants their place shot properly before it hits the market.

What we actually do

Everything a listing needs, in one place. Photography, twilight shoots, drone, day-to-dusk conversions, walkthrough video, social reels, 2D and 3D floor plans, and virtual staging. Most studios do one or two of these well. We do all of them, every shoot, fast.

The team

There's a small group of us — three part-time photographers (plus me, Jason, still shooting most days), a coordinator handling the scheduling and admin side, and an editor working through the back end so galleries land in your inbox quickly.

Keeping the team tight means every shoot looks consistent. You're not getting a different style every time you book.

What makes us different

Speed. We turn galleries around fast — usually next-day delivery. In a market where listings go live in 48 hours, that matters more than people pretend it doesn't.

Quality. Every property gets full coverage — the wide angles, the natural light, the exterior shot at the right time of day. We've shot enough Perth properties to know what sells them.

Easy to deal with. Online booking, instant confirmation, no chasing. Your photographer turns up on time with everything sorted. No calls asking where to park.

Local. We're in Perth, we know Perth, we know what works for Perth properties. Subiaco is not the Hills is not South Perth.

How a shoot with us works

  1. Book online. Pick a package or single service, a date and the address. Confirmation is instant, and you can book Monday to Saturday.
  2. We turn up ready. A sales photography shoot is about 60 minutes on site; packages run 90 to 150 minutes depending on what’s included. You don’t need to be there if we have a lockbox, key or the agent letting us in.
  3. We shoot the whole property. Interiors, exteriors, detail shots and, where the package includes them, aerials, the floor plan measure and the 360° tour — in one visit.
  4. The gallery lands next day. Photography, drone and floor plans are delivered the next day; video takes two days because of the edit.

Prices are the same anywhere in the metro area, GST included, with no travel loading — the full list is on the pricing page.

Who we work with

Most of our work is for sales agents, but the same crew covers the rest of the property market:

  • Sales listings — see what a shoot with a Perth real estate photographer includes
  • Property managers — rental photography built around tenants and entry notice
  • Short-stay hosts — Airbnb and holiday-let photography
  • Offices, shops and warehouses — commercial property photography, quoted per job
  • Agents themselves — agent headshots that match across a team
  • Homeowners selling privately or prepping a home for an agent — same services, same prices

How we keep the work honest

Listing images have to show the property as it is. Every image we virtually stage or declutter is marked as virtually enhanced when we deliver it — our guide to virtual staging disclosure explains why. Aerial work is flown in line with CASA’s rules for commercial drone operation, and we check addresses near Perth Airport and Jandakot before promising aerials (see drone rules for real estate in WA).

We also write plain-English guides for agents and sellers — floor plans, drone rules, the best time of day to shoot — so you can judge any photographer’s work, not just ours.

Where we work

Perth metro and surrounds — from Alkimos to Rockingham, the coast to the Hills. Our real estate photographer Perth page sets out exactly what a sales shoot includes and costs. If you're not sure whether your patch is in our area, check every suburb we cover or just ask.

Questions about us

Who runs Baker Production?

Jason Baker, who still shoots most days. He works with three part-time photographers, a coordinator who handles scheduling and admin, and an editor, so every gallery is shot and edited to the same standard.

Where is Baker Production based?

Perth, Western Australia. We cover the metro area from Alkimos in the north to Rockingham in the south and from the coast to the Perth Hills, at the same price everywhere. Mandurah, Peel and regional WA are quoted individually.

How fast do we get our photos?

Photography, drone and floor plans are delivered the next day. Listing video takes two days. If you need something sooner, ask — we can usually find a way.

Do you only work with real estate agents?

No. Agents are most of our work, but we also shoot for property managers, Airbnb hosts, commercial landlords and homeowners. The services and prices are the same.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Overcast and light rain are fine for interiors, and exteriors get a sky replacement. If it is genuinely too rough — heavy rain or storms — we reschedule at no extra cost.

How do we pay?

Card through the booking system is the default. Bank transfer works too, and agencies can be invoiced on standard terms. A $110 fee applies only to cancellations within one hour of the appointment.
