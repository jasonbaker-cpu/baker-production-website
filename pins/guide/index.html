Drone edit hand-back

For Profocus. What to do with Baker Production drone photos once the sky and colour are done. Everything happens in the browser; nothing gets emailed or uploaded to Dropbox. You will get an email titled "Drone edit needed" with the links for each job.

Before you start

  • Edit only the sky, colour and horizon. Add no pins, text, logos or boundary lines.
  • Don't crop or rotate the photos.
  • Keep the original file names, for example DJI_20261030214947_0368_D.jpg. That's how each photo finds its pins.
  • Export full-size JPGs.
  • Natural colour — don't over-saturate. Clean whites, real greens, a bright natural blue sky. If anything looks even a little off (haze, ghosting, blotchy sky), fix it.
  • Top-down shots: full frame, exactly as shot. No turning, no mirrored or blurred edges.
  • Upload drone photos here, not to Dropbox Finals. No need to check the pins — Baker's team does that.
1

Open the job link from the email

The link looks like bakerproduction.com/pins/?open=30-oct-2026/1_monk_pl. Our version of the job opens with its boundary, pins and the agent's booking notes, highlighted, top right.

2

Drag all your edited photos onto the page

Select every finished JPG and drop them anywhere on the editor, or click Add photos. A message says "Editor's photos swapped in". The photo count stays the same, because your photos replace ours and keep every pin, label and the boundary.

If the count goes up instead (for example 8 becomes 16), a file name was changed. Rename it back to the original and try again.

3

Click Send to Baker

Top right, next to Export all. Type your name when asked. The photos save straight to Baker, so you don't need to download or send any files. Wait for "Sent to Baker. Thanks, you're done."

4

That's it

Baker's team opens the same job, sees your photos with the pins on top, double-checks everything and delivers it.
