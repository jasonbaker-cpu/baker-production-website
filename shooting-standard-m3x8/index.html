Rules

Camera setup

  • Tripod for every interior. No handheld brackets.
  • Level gauge on. Camera level left-right and front-back on every shot. The editor can't fix a tilted camera without cropping.
  • Verticals straight in camera. Door frames and wall corners should stand straight on the screen.
  • Height: about benchtop to chest height (1.1-1.4m). Lower in small rooms. Never eye height looking down.
  • Lens: 16-24mm on full frame (10-16mm on APS-C). Not wider: rooms stretch and look fake.
  • Settings: f/8, ISO 100-200, 2s timer, same bracket set on every shot.

Brackets

  • Same number of frames every scene (5 at 2 stops is our default).
  • Darkest frame must show the window view with no white blowout. If it's still white, add a darker frame.
  • Brightest frame must show detail in the dark corners.
  • Don't touch the tripod until the last frame is done.
  • Every scene bracketed, exteriors included.

Framing & coverage

  • One wide hero shot of every main room, from the corner into the room. Never just from the doorway.
  • Second angle for living, kitchen, meals and master.
  • Don't shoot the same angle twice. Move the tripod, don't just turn it.
  • Exterior: front straight-on plus a 3/4 angle, rear yard, alfresco, pool.

Before you press the shutter

  • Lights on and all globes working (premium day: lights off).
  • Blinds open evenly, TVs and screens off, toilet lids down.
  • Check mirrors and glass for you or the tripod.
  • Cars, bins and hoses out of the front shots.
  • Follow the booking notes.
  • Same day: upload to Raw Files/your name/date/address and check the file count before wiping the card.
