Video is what an agent uses to win the next listing

A photo set sells the property. A video sells the agent. Every vendor in the street sees the reel that runs on your feed, and the seller two doors down decides whether you are the person who markets homes properly or the person who takes a few photos and hopes.

Baker Production shoots and edits property video for Perth agents in three formats, because a portal listing, an Instagram feed and a vendor pitch are three different jobs. A full listing video is a considered cinematic edit for the campaign. A social walkthrough is a fast vertical piece built for the feed. A day-to-night edit is the premium option for homes where the light changes the story — a rear elevation onto the river, a pool, an outlook.

Everything is shot with stabilised motion, cut to music we are licensed to use, and graded to match the stills from the same shoot, so the campaign looks like one piece of work rather than three suppliers.

Delivered in web, social and portal-ready formats.
