Listing Video Music

Pick the song for your listing video. Filter by the feel of the home, click any title to have a listen on YouTube, then send us the song number when you book (or reply to your booking email). Songs marked ★ feature in films from Australia's top agencies.

For editors: so it never gets blocked on YouTube: post it as a Short (under 3 min), export the video with no music, then add the song in the YouTube app with Add sound. If the song shows up in Add sound, it's licensed for Shorts. Never put a label song in during the edit and upload it, because a Short over 1 minute with a Content ID claim gets blocked worldwide. For full-length videos, upload as Unlisted first and only publish if Copyright checks comes back clear.
Log every song you use, so the same agent doesn't get the same track twice.
