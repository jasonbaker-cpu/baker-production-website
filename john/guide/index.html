The goal

When a Coogee homeowner decides to sell, John is the first agent they think of, because they've already watched him walk through a dozen homes on their street.

Followers and likes aren't the score. Appraisals booked and listings won from Instagram are.

The edge is simple: most agents post a listing and go quiet. John shows up every week, in the same suburbs, with real homes and real numbers.

Making each video

Instagram ranks reels on how long people watch and how often they send them to a friend. So every video has two jobs: hold people to the end, and be worth forwarding.

  • One hook, first two seconds. Open on the best thing in the home, or the one number that surprises. No "Hey guys, John here."
  • Walk and talk. Moving through the home holds attention better than standing still.
  • Never criticise a vendor's home. Put the honesty on the buyer: "Not for you if you want a big backyard."
  • WA terms: home open, offers, appraisal.
  • Length: walkthroughs 30–60 seconds, talking heads under 40.
  • Grey-box captions burned in. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • Instagram caption: one or two lines. No hashtag walls.

End every video the same way:

"Thinking of selling in Coogee? Message me. John O'Brien, Raspa."

Turning views into listings

  1. The profile sells. Name: "John O'Brien | Coogee Real Estate". Bio with his suburbs and "Book an appraisal ↓". A clean headshot. Highlights: Sold, Suburbs, Reviews.
  2. Every video asks for the appraisal.
  3. Reply the same day to every comment and DM. Each one is a possible appraisal.
  4. Capture the lead in the CRM that day, with suburb and timing.
  5. Follow up within 24 hours, then every few weeks until they're ready.
  6. Show the videos in every listing presentation: "This is how your home will be marketed."

Offline too: a QR code to his Instagram on letterbox drops, signboards and home open sheets.

The rules

  1. Never claim a sale he didn't make. Port Coogee is location content until he's sold there.
  2. Never criticise a vendor's home on camera.
  3. Every number is real: REIWA or his own results.
  4. Stay in the patch. Off-patch listings go up, but never get pinned.
  5. Never miss a week.
  6. One hook per video. No American gimmicks.
  7. Every video ends on the appraisal ask.
  8. Get the vendor's OK before filming their home.
