Questions owners ask

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation to list. Many owners use it to plan a move that is a year or two away.

Do you need to see inside?

Not for a first range. For a sharper figure, a short walk-through makes a real difference, and John only books one if you want it.

What does off-market mean?

Your home is shown privately to buyers on John's register before, or instead of, going online. You see what serious buyers would pay with no public campaign.

Will my details be shared?

No. They go to John at Raspa Property Group only, and are only used to contact you about your property.
