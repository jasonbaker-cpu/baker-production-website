For the buyer who cannot get to the home open

Perth sells a lot of property to people who are not in Perth on a Saturday morning. East-coast buyers, expats, FIFO workers on swing, investors who will never see the place. A 360-degree tour lets them walk it properly instead of guessing from twelve photographs.

It also changes who shows up. An agent running a tour on the listing gets fewer tyre-kickers and more genuinely interested inspections, because the people who were never going to like the layout have already worked that out at home.

Captured during the same visit as the photography, delivered as a link, and it works on a phone.
