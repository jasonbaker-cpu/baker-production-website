What to say

Learn the shape, say it your way. Never apologise for the gap.

Past client — 2 weeks to a month quiet

"Hey [name], Jason from Baker Production — how've you been? … Listen, I was going back through my jobs and realised I hadn't shot anything for you since [address]. How did that one end up going?"

Then shut up and listen. That's the whole call.

"Can I ask you straight — was there anything about how we did it that didn't suit you? I'd rather know than wonder."

"I'd love to shoot for you again. Booking spring dates now — anything coming up? I'll throw in a social walkthrough video on the next one, on me."

Long gap — 4 to 6 months (Celeste, Don, trial shoots)

"Hey [name], it's Jason from Baker Production — the photographer who shot [address] for you back in [month]. It's been a while! How've you been?"

"It's been a busy few months, been flat out. I'm locking in spring dates now and thought I'd give you a call rather than send another email."

Or skip the gap entirely: "I'm getting my spring calendar sorted and you were on my list. What have you got coming up?"

Name one real detail from that property — proves it isn't a mass call.

"Send me your next listing and I'll do the walkthrough video on me, so you can see if it's still up to scratch."

Book the follow-up before you hang up. A long gap closes on the second or third call, not the first.

Cold agent — free declutter hook

"Hi [name], it's Jason from Baker Production — I'm a real estate photographer here in Perth. Have you got twenty seconds?"

"I was looking at your listing on [street] — it presents really well, only thing pulling it down is a bit of clutter in a couple of shots. I run a decluttering tool so I cleaned two of your photos up. No charge, no catch — want me to send them?"

"When you shoot with me the decluttering's included free on every job — it's not an add-on."

"Anything coming to market in the next couple of weeks? Even if it's not locked in I'll pencil you in."

Send the images the same day or the call was wasted.

Same agency as an existing client

"Hi [name], it's Jason from Baker Production — I do the photography for [colleague] in your office. Got a minute?"

"I've been shooting their listings for months, so I already know your office's floorplan style and branding — logo on the web images, clean high-res, all of it."

"Send me your next listing and have a look at what I did for [colleague] at [address] first — that'll tell you more than I can."

Never say the colleague recommended you unless they did. "I shoot for them" is true and does the same job.

When they push back

"We've already got a photographer."
"Course you do — everyone does. Not asking you to swap, just keep me for when they're booked out. Can I send my price list so I'm in your phone?"

"Send me an email."
"Will do. Just so I send the right thing — mostly units or houses?" (Then actually send it, same day.)

"Nothing on at the moment."
"All good — that's why I'm calling early. When you do get one, what turnaround do you need? … I'll check back in a fortnight, alright?"

"How much?"
Flat, no apology: "$299 Essential — photos, floorplan, decluttering included. Most go Premium at $375 with the drone. What sort of properties are you listing?"

"You're dearer than X."
"Probably am. What you get for it is the decluttering and the turnaround. Try me on one and judge it on the photos."

"Bad time."
"No worries — when's better, morning or arvo?" Then hang up.

Awkward pause
Let it sit. Whoever speaks first usually loses.

Voicemail + follow-up text

"Hi [name], Jason from Baker Production, real estate photographer in Perth — 0466 789 522. I've got a couple of spring slots left and wanted to offer you one. I'll flick you a text as well. Thanks!"

Text: "Hi [name], Jason from Baker Production — just left you a voicemail. Real estate photography, decluttering included free. Anything coming to market soon?"

Under 15 seconds. Say the number twice. The text is what gets replied to.

Before you dial

Stand up. Smile. Their listing open on screen. Target is dials, not wins — voicemail counts. Warm ones first. Bullets, not a script. Same hour every day, 9–10am or 3–4pm.
