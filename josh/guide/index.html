Making each video

  • One hook, first two seconds, and say "Heathridge" in the first line. The big TikTok Josh Carroll owns the name, so Josh owns "Josh Carroll Heathridge".
  • Walk and talk. Talking points, not a script.
  • Never criticise a vendor's home. Put the honesty on the buyer: "Not for you if you want a big backyard."
  • WA terms: home open, offers, appraisal.
  • Captions burned in. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • Instagram caption: one line. No hashtag walls.

End every video with one ask:

"Thinking of selling in Heathridge? DM me 'HEATHRIDGE' and I'll send you what's sold on your street."

The filming routine. Listing walk-and-talks are filmed at each listing shoot. Local and market reels are batched on a Baker shoot day: Thu 26 Nov, Thu 17 Dec, Thu 21 Jan.

Turning views into listings

  1. Fix the profile. Pin Caprice plus two other strong reels. Move the award tiles to a highlight. Bio link: bakerproduction.com/josh-carroll.
  2. Every video asks for the "HEATHRIDGE" DM.
  3. Reply within the hour to every comment and DM. The Caprice reel had a buyer asking for details in the comments.
  4. Capture the lead in the Heathridge app the same day. Website leads land there by themselves.
  5. Show the videos in every listing presentation: "This is how your home will be marketed."
  6. Every listing feeds the next. Letterbox the 50–100 homes around each listing with a QR to the reel.
