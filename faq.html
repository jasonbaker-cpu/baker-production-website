Yes, provided it is disclosed. Australian Consumer Law prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct and false representations about property, so a staged image must be identifiable as enhanced rather than presented as the room as it stands. Baker Studio marks every enhanced image automatically. This is general information, not legal advice — check your agency's own policy as well.
Yes. Aerial work is flown in line with CASA's rules for commercial drone operation, including the standard conditions on altitude, distance from people and controlled airspace. Locations near Perth Airport or Jandakot need approval, which we arrange before the shoot.
Yes. Plans are drawn from measurements taken during the shoot rather than traced from a builder's original, so they reflect extensions, renovations and removed walls.
